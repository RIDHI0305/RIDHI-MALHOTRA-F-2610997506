About Us :

Established in 1997 by the Chitkara Educational Trust, Chitkara University is a leading institution of higher education in India. The university offers a wide range of undergraduate and postgraduate programs in various disciplines, including engineering, management, pharmacy, and health sciences.



Recognised for academic excellence,Chitkara University holds an NAAC A+ accreditation and is approved by the University Grants Commission (UGC). The university has a strong focus on research and innovation, with state-of-the-art laboratories and research centers that facilitate cutting-edge research in various fields.
